How to play

  • Protect the house. Storm husks pour out of the purple portals. If the house or you fall, the wave fails.
  • Left thumb moves. Right thumb drags to aim. Hold FIRE (you can aim while holding it). Auto-fire can shoot for you when your crosshair is on an enemy.
  • Waves keep coming. Clear one and the next starts automatically. Your gun, hero and pet are locked in until you fall; then you're back at the base to swap gear, recruit, and build.
  • Guns, heroes and pets level up as you use them, all the way to level 160. Each new rarity (Uncommon, Rare, Epic, Legendary, Mythic) unlocks a new ability.
  • Every hero has an ability button (grenade, dash, shield, turret, heal or lightning) plus perks.
  • Collect coins to recruit heroes, unlock guns, adopt pets and build walls, traps and turret towers in Edit Base. The house grows stronger as you reach higher waves.
  • Red arrows and beams show where the next enemies will come from. Check the minimap!
  • 2 PLAYER: host a game and send your friend the code. They open the same game link, tap 2 PLAYER and type it in.
  • Failed a wave? You keep every coin. Upgrade and try that wave again.
  • Every 5th wave a Storm King boss appears. Aim for the head for bonus damage!
