How to play

  • Protect the house. Storm husks pour out of the purple portals. If the house or you fall, the wave fails.
  • Left thumb moves. Right thumb drags to aim. Hold FIRE (you can aim while holding it). Auto-fire can shoot for you when your crosshair is on an enemy.
  • Collect coins from defeated enemies, then spend them at the base: upgrade guns, buy new ones, build barricades, turrets and spike traps.
  • Failed a wave? You keep every coin. Upgrade and try that wave again.
  • Every 5th wave a Storm King boss appears. Aim for the head for bonus damage!
